## Deliverable 1 — Perceptual Stability Review

### Why This Split Matters for Expanding the Umwelt

Umwelt, in the biological sense, is the perceptual world an organism actually inhabits — not the universe in full, but the slice of reality its senses and cognition can reach. xPRIMEray has always been an instrument for *widening* that slice: curved null-geodesic traversal through Gordon effective metrics, GRIN fields, observer disagreement diagnostics, hermetic closure validation. These are not decorative shaders. They are ways of walking paths that straight-ray intuition cannot.

Today, mathematical truth and visualization share a single Godot body. That coupling was necessary for birth — the engine learned to see by rendering. But it also limits who can walk the paths. A Python researcher cannot drop a new GRIN profile without opening a game engine. A web visitor cannot feel the wormhole bend without installing Godot. A contributor who thinks in fixtures and golden images must wade through scene graphs to touch the transport core.

**Project Glowing Heart** extracts the mathematical heart — the transport observatory's truth layer — into **xPRIMEray Core**, while keeping the Godot edition as the primary **creative telescope** and MisterY Labs as the **public observatory**.

This is an umwelt expansion in three directions at once:

| Layer | Role after split | Umwelt gain |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| **Core** | Language-agnostic transport truth: integrators, fields, fixtures, validation | Researchers, CLI tools, WASM modules, future engines |
| **Godot Edition** | Walkable worlds, shaders, mythos dressing, dolly exports | Creators, experiencers, cinematic intuition |
| **MisterY Labs** | Observatory Atlas, curated artifacts, visitor curriculum | Anyone with twenty minutes and a browser |

Separating mathematical truth from visualization does not diminish either. It lets more minds walk the same curved paths through different doors — and compare what they see through shared observables (pixel disagreement counts, closure percentages, curvature heat maps) rather than arguing from incompatible intuitions.

The split is stable when both halves remain honest about what they are: Core does not pretend to be beautiful; Godot does not pretend to be the sole source of truth. The observatory holds the conversation between them.

### Philosophical / Mythic Framing

#### The Bee Sigil — Witness, Not Logo

The bee B/q sigil in the Optical Transport Illusion demo is procedural geometry in a shader — hex lattice, center glyph, wing flutter. It is also a **witness mark**: the sign that something is *looking* through the portal, that transport is being observed rather than merely displayed.

In the Broch Sphere helix, xPRIMEray sits at the tip of the Knowledge Lineage strand — Faraday's field lines made traversable, Maxwell's equations made visible, Einstein's observer-geometry made interactive. The bee is the Story Lineage's answer: *we are here, seeing together*. It should survive the split as a **perceptual anchor**, not a branding sticker. Core carries the witness orbs (RK4 integrators tracing real field samples). Godot carries the sigil shimmer on the portal frame. Both say: *light took a path; we measured it.*

#### The Wormhole as Perceptual Vehicle

A wormhole in xPRIMEray is not a science-fiction prop. It is a **Gordon-metric throat** — a region where refractive index gradients bend null geodesics so dramatically that inside and outside become optically inseparable without diagnostics. Chapter 1 of the Observatory Atlas asks the right question: *Is this bending real, or a rendering trick?* The Dual Reality inset answers by showing curved and straight transport side by side on identical geometry.

The wormhole is therefore a **perceptual vehicle**: a structure that carries the observer across a boundary between what they expected to see and what the field actually does. Walking through the Godot portal demo is the embodied version. Running the wormhole witness fixture through Core is the instrumented version. Neither replaces the other. The vehicle only works when both exist.

#### Evolution Beyond Darwin — Deeper Knowing

The Broch Helix crosses Darwin's evolution strand with speculative fiction: evidence generates story, story generates new questions that return to evidence. But Glowing Heart proposes something the execution plan will not say aloud: **evolution of perception is not the same as evolution of species.**

Darwin gave us descent with modification. xPRIMEray gives us **transport with disagreement** — the measurable gap between what curved and straight observers see (23.8% pixel classification delta at off-axis pose in the observer disagreement experiment). That gap is not error. It is the engine's thesis rendered as a number.

"Deeper knowing" here means the Brothers Karamazov confrontation: not the comfortable answer, but the moment Ivan and Alyosha sit with the horror and the wonder simultaneously. Hermetic closure at budget=32 looks plausible and reports 0% closure. That is the engine refusing to let you look away. The split must preserve this honesty — Core owns the uncomfortable number; Godot owns the breath when you cross the portal; the observatory owns the curriculum that teaches you why both matter.

### Risk / Benefit from Human Perspective

#### Creators (Godot artists, shader authors, demo builders)

**Gain:** Freedom to iterate on mythos, pacing, and walkability without risking transport correctness. Wire live Core samples into `grin_field_sampler.gd` instead of sinusoidal stubs. Ship dolly animations and Suno-synced exports without touching integrator math.

**Lose:** The frictionless "change anything anywhere" workflow. Boundaries between Core API and Godot adapter must be respected. Mitigation: golden fixture parity tests — if the portal looks right but Core disagrees, Godot is wrong, full stop.

#### Researchers (physicists, optical engineers, diagnostic authors)

**Gain:** Drop GRIN profiles, Gordon metrics, and fixture definitions without opening the Godot editor. Reproduce wormhole checkpoint sequences, observer disagreement sweeps, and cathedral probe diagnostics from CLI or notebook. Publish methods independent of engine version.

**Lose:** Immediate visual feedback inside the authoring environment. Mitigation: Phase 7+ web/WASM preview of Core fixtures; observatory artifact pipeline auto-publishes renders from Core runs.

#### Experiencers (Atlas visitors, demo walkers, video viewers)

**Gain:** Clearer trust ladder — "this number comes from Core" vs. "this feeling comes from Godot." The MVO arc (wonder → measurement → validation) gains a fourth implicit step: *reproducibility across surfaces.*

**Lose:** The magic of "it's all one thing." A child walking through the portal may not care about repos. Mitigation: **never expose the split in the first eight minutes.** The observatory leads with wormhole beauty; the architecture reveal is for contributors.

#### The Project Itself

**Risk:** Split becomes silo — Core team optimizes benchmarks, Godot team optimizes vibes, observatory stalemates between them.

**Benefit:** Split becomes **stereopsis** — two eyes on the same transport truth, depth perception through disagreement. The project's immune system (hermetic closure, observer disagreement, coherence basin maps) only works if Core and Godot are forced to look at the same fixtures.

## Deliverable 2 — Rocket-Sled Recommendations

*Concrete perceptual-impact enhancements for Phases 0–8. Align with `project_glowing_heart_execution_plan.md`; escalate conflicts to the engineering review.*

### Phase 0 — Boundary Drawing & Inventory

- **Name the heart aloud.** Publish a one-page "What Lives in Core" diagram alongside the technical inventory: integrators, `FieldSystem`, transport ownership, oracle probes, fixture protocol. Pair every engineering boundary with a **visitor sentence** ("This is the part that decides whether a pixel lied").
- **Fixture-first folklore.** Catalog existing golden fixtures (`test-overspace-wormhole-witness-fixture`, hermetic closure scenes, atomic orbital GRIN ladder) as *mythic anchors* — name them in observatory copy, not just commit hashes.

### Phase 1 — Core API Surface & Package Skeleton

- **Witness Orb API.** Expose the RK4 geodesic integrator (already prototyped in `rk4_witness_orb.gd`) as the canonical "hello world" — a single ray through a radial GRIN well, JSON telemetry out. This is the bee's mathematical wingbeat.
- **Branding seed:** `xprimeray-core` repo subtitle: *"The transport truth layer."* Avoid "library" or "SDK" — those words kill wonder.

### Phase 2 — Transport Math Extraction

- **Parity demo scene.** Before deleting Godot copies, run wormhole witness fixture through both stacks; publish the observer disagreement delta as the phase exit gate. Make the number visible on the observatory.
- **GRIN profile gallery.** Ship three bundled profiles with human names: *Throat*, *Well*, *Orbital* — mapped to existing research fixtures.

### Phase 3 — Fixture Protocol & Golden Images

- **Atlas Chapter 1 in a fixture file.** Encode the Dual Reality comparison as a Core fixture manifest so any renderer adapter must pass the same six-panel story.
- **Short-form video:** 15-second "same scene, two transports" split-screen for social — pulled automatically from golden image diff.

### Phase 4 — Observer Disagreement Pipeline

- **Interactive observatory widget.** Embed the 30,839-pixel delta story (Chapter 2) as a live slider: camera pose in, disagreement ratio out — fed by Core, rendered by Godot or static WebGL.
- **Researcher win:** `xprimeray-core disagree --fixture wormhole --pose offaxis` prints the ratio in under 5 seconds.

### Phase 5 — Hermetic Validation Harness

- **The cliff as onboarding.** First-run Core experience: deliberately show budget=32 vs budget=700 side by side. New contributors must see 0% vs 100% closure before they get write access metaphors.
- **Observatory Chapter 3 badge.** "Survived the Hermetic Cliff" — playful, but points to real validation literacy.

### Phase 6 — Godot Adapter Layer

- **Wire, don't rewrite.** Replace `grin_field_sampler.gd` sinusoidal stub with live Core texture feed; keep bee sigil, peacock shimmer, J&B pillars untouched in shader.
- **Magic preservation rule:** No Core dependency in the player controller, portal trigger, or dolly animation — only in transport truth paths. Walking must still feel like walking.
- **Dual-repo dev loop document:** "Godot points at Core build artifact" one-liner for contributors.

### Phase 7 — Web / WASM / Secondary Surfaces

- **Minimum Glowing Heart web demo:** Core WASM module renders a single wormhole witness frame in canvas; toggle curved/straight; show disagreement percentage. No install. Atlas embed target.
- **Observatory integration:** New homepage callout card linking Godot demo + web demo + Core repo — three doors, one wormhole.

### Phase 8 — Public Launch & Observatory Integration

- **First public "Glowing Heart" demo package:**
  1. **Core fixture:** `glowing_heart_witness_v1` — radial GRIN well + single throat crossing; golden PNG + JSON telemetry.
  2. **Godot lens:** Optical Transport Illusion portal with live Core-fed `grin_field` texture and RK4 orbs synced to Core stepping.
  3. **Web lens:** WASM render of the same fixture with Dual Reality toggle.
  4. **Observatory lens:** Atlas sidebar explaining: "Same transport, three telescopes."
- **Short-form launch sequence:** Three 30-second videos — "The Number" (disagreement), "The Cliff" (hermetic), "The Walk" (portal crossing). Sync to observatory chapter releases.
- **MisterY Labs Media gallery:** Tag artifacts with `source:core`, `source:godot`, `source:observatory` so provenance is visible without being loud.

### Keeping Godot Alive and Magical During the Split

1. **Mythos immunity.** Bee sigil, peacock queen accents, Fractured Storm Jester noclip, "enter the bulk" — these are Godot edition identity. Core does not absorb them.
2. **Weekly dolly exports.** Maintain `create_optical_dolly_animation()` exports even during extraction sprints — the project's emotional heartbeat is motion through the portal.
3. **Shader recursion first.** Prioritize SubViewport depth and trap-to-bloom color grade over refactors; people feel recursion before they read integrator source.
4. **Live orbs as truth ambassadors.** RK4 witness orbs remain the bridge object — they literally follow Core math while glowing in Godot space.
5. **No "deprecated wonder."** If a feature moves to Core, Godot gains a *better* visualization of it the same week. Never a removal without a bloom.
