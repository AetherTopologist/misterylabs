# Epistemic labels

Reusable public language. These are **not** decoration.
Every public exhibit that leaves established physics must wear at least one.

Do not invent synonyms that sound more certain.

| Label | Meaning | May claim | Must not claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ESTABLISHED PHYSICS** | Textbook / consensus model with broad observational support | This is the working scientific baseline | That it is finished or without open questions |
| **OBSERVATION** | Measured quantity or well-replicated dataset | Neutrinos, helioseismology, photospheric T, magnetar B | A unique interpretation of the data |
| **OPEN QUESTION** | Active research inside established physics | Coronal heating, solar metallicity/opacity tension | That an exotic model is required |
| **ALTERNATIVE MODEL** | Historically or popularly proposed competitor | What the model asserts | That it is equally evidenced |
| **PAIS-INSPIRED** | Drawn from Pais patents/papers as written | What the source actually says | That the Navy validated the physics |
| **MATHEMATICAL ANALOGY** | Same equations, different ontology | Useful mapping (e.g. Gordon metric ↔ dielectric) | Identity of the two systems |
| **MISTERY LABS EXTRAPOLATION** | Our step beyond the source | A testable “if we assume X” question | That Pais, NASA, or Nature said it |
| **TOY MODEL** | Intentionally reduced dynamics | Pedagogical behavior | Predictive astrophysics |
| **SPECULATIVE VISUALIZATION** | Art / field-glyph of an assumed configuration | “Looks like this under assumption Y” | Evidence |
| **ART DIRECTION** | Illuminated-manuscript / Renaissance-science motif | Mood, iconography | Historical authenticity or portrait-as-fact |

## Chain (must stay visible)

```
SOURCE
→ ESTABLISHED OBSERVATION
→ INTERPRETATION
→ HYPOTHESIS
→ SIMULATION ASSUMPTION
→ PREDICTED OBSERVABLE
```

A simulation is **not** a claim that nature works that way.
