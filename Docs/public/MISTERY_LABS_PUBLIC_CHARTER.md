# MisterY Labs Public Charter

## Mission

Make difficult physical ideas visible, manipulable, and inspectable.

xPRIMEray is the upstream scientific instrument.
MisterY Labs is the public laboratory surrounding it.

## Public interaction contract

Design mobile-first.

A new visitor should:
- understand what can be manipulated in ~5 seconds;
- produce a meaningful visual change in ~10 seconds;
- learn primarily by interacting rather than reading.

Desktop may expose deeper instrumentation.
Mobile must not be a crippled desktop interface.

## Scientific contract

Separate:
- model behavior,
- observation,
- interpretation,
- speculation.

Never present speculative physical interpretation as experimental evidence.

xPRIMEray engine authority is upstream.
Public demonstrations may explain it.
They may not redefine its semantics.

## Attribution contract

Inspiration is not anonymous raw material.

For externally inspired work:
- identify the original creator when reasonably possible;
- distinguish original sources from reposts;
- preserve the source link;
- state what inspired us;
- state what MisterY Labs changed or extended;
- direct visitors back to the creator.

If provenance cannot be established:
ORIGINAL CREATOR: UNRESOLVED

Never manufacture attribution.

## Visual language

Target:
science museum + optical bench + living research notebook.

Prefer:
- real geometry;
- visible paths;
- direct manipulation;
- clean scientific diagrams;
- neutral backgrounds;
- concise explanation.

Avoid:
- fake scientific HUDs;
- generic cyberpunk;
- decorative glowing grids;
- unnecessary interface chrome;
- walls of prerequisite text.

## Demo principle

Every exhibit should answer:

"What misconception does interaction dissolve?"

Earn complexity progressively.

Do not begin the public journey with exotic geometry.
